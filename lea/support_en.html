Frequently asked questions

Are my quotes sent to the publisher?

No. Quotes, invoices, customers and media stay on your phone. See the privacy policy for the few features that use the network.

Where is my data if I change phones?

There is no online account. Use the app's encrypted exportable backup to move your data.

Are photo or AR measurements exact?

They are estimates that you must check and confirm.

Report a problem

Please give your device model, system version, app version and a description of what happened. Do not send customer or other sensitive data.
