1. Who we are

Controller: Romain Robert (Brume & Pixel). Privacy contact: romain.robert2@outlook.fr.

2. In short

Léa - Devis Pro helps tradespeople create and keep quotes, invoices, customers, services, signatures and job-site information. This data is stored on your phone and is not sent to the publisher. There is no online account, no advertising, no sale of data and no tracking across apps or websites.

A few features, detailed below, use a third-party service or the network: weather (after your confirmation), optional statistics (after your consent), the system voice dictation, Google ML Kit components, and, if you turn it on, a Mac or an Ollama AI on your local network.

3. Data kept on your device

Quotes, invoices, customers, services, signatures, photos, job-site videos and settings stay in the app, on your phone. You choose freely whether to export, print or share a document; the exported copy is then no longer under the app's control. Depending on your settings, system backups may include the local data; job-site videos are excluded from the automatic backups managed by the app. You can also create an exportable backup encrypted with a password.

4. Device permissions

  • Camera, photo library (photos you pick) and microphone: only after an action by you (taking a photo, filming, measuring, adding a logo, dictating). Media stays local unless you share or export it.
  • Speech recognition: see section 6.
  • Approximate location: only when you request the weather and after your confirmation (section 7). No background tracking.
  • Local network: only if you choose to pair your Mac or ask a local Ollama AI for a suggestion (section 9).
  • Bluetooth (Android): to use a headset for dictation. Internet and network state: for the features described below.
  • Augmented reality sensors (ARCore or ARKit) are used for photo or AR measurements, which are estimates you must check.

The app does not access your contacts, text messages, calendar or general storage.

5. Image analysis (Google ML Kit)

Text and object recognition uses Google ML Kit. The image and its result are processed on your device. Google states, however, that this component collects technical information: phone model and version, app version, installation identifier, configuration, events, errors and performance. It is used for operation, diagnostics and usage analytics, is encrypted in transit, and is not used by the publisher for advertising or tracking.

6. Voice dictation

The app uses the system speech recognition service only when you start dictation. It keeps no audio recording. The system provider (Apple or Google) may process the voice on the device or remotely depending on your phone, language and settings.

7. Weather (MET Norway), optional

After your confirmation, the app asks for your location while in use. Latitude and longitude, rounded to 2 decimals, are sent over HTTPS to the MET Norway weather service, which also receives your IP address and the app's public identifier. If you entered an optional weather contact in the company settings, it is also sent in the User-Agent header. No automatic requests are made. You can decline.

8. Optional statistics

After explicit consent, the app sends aggregated counters showing that certain features worked. No customer, document, amount, text, media, address, account identifier or stable identifier is included. The packet contains the platform, closed counters and a temporary random token. This data is sent over HTTPS to Cloudflare infrastructure used by the publisher to improve the app. You can decline or withdraw consent in the app; counters still on the phone are then erased. Aggregates that have already been anonymised cannot be deleted individually.

9. Mac or local AI (optional)

If you pair your Mac or ask an Ollama AI for a suggestion, the app communicates with that device on your local network. The content sent stays on your network and is not transmitted to the publisher.

10. Payment and e-invoicing

You may enter a payment link; the app only checks that it uses HTTPS and does not call it. The provider you choose applies its own privacy policy. The app is not an approved e-invoicing platform and does not automatically file invoices in this version.

11. Deletion

Documents and media can be deleted from the app. Uninstalling removes the local storage, subject to system backups or exports you chose to keep. No online account is created.

12. Intended audience

The app is intended for professionals. It shows no advertising, does not sell data and does not track users across apps or websites.

13. Your rights

For any question or request (access, correction, erasure) about data the publisher may hold, for example your support messages, write to romain.robert2@outlook.fr. You may also contact the supervisory authority of your country (in France: the CNIL, cnil.fr).

14. Changes

This policy will be updated before any version that handles data differently is released.
