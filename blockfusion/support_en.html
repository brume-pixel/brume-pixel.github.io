FAQ

Is the game free?

No: it is a one-time purchase, with no ads, no subscription and no in-app purchases. The price shown is the store's.

Do I need an Internet connection?

No, the game works fully offline.

Where is my progress saved?

Only on your device. There is no account and no sync: if you uninstall the game or change device, progress is not transferred automatically.

I cannot find my purchase or I changed device.

Re-download the game from your store account (App Store or Google Play). There is no "Restore" button because the game manages no purchases.

Refunds

Refunds are handled by Apple or Google under their rules; the publisher cannot refund directly.

Is the game available in my language?

For now, French only.

Report a problem

Please include your device model, system version (Android or iOS), game version and a description of what happened (steps, screenshot if possible). Do not send sensitive data.
