1. Who we are

Publisher: Romain Robert (Brume & Pixel). Contact: romain.robert2@outlook.fr.

2. In short

Block Fusion does not collect, transmit or share any personal data. The game has no account, no advertising, no tracking tool, no usage analytics and no in-app purchases. It works without an Internet connection and requests no network permission.

3. What is stored on your device

To work, the game stores information only in the app's private storage on your device: your progress (records, levels, statistics), your settings and the resume state of the game. The game never sends it to the publisher or to any third party, and it is deleted when you uninstall the game. It contains no name, address, advertising identifier or device identifier.

4. Device permissions

The game only uses the device's vibration, which can be turned off in settings. It requests no access to the camera, microphone, location, contacts, photos or the Internet.

5. Purchase

The game is sold as a one-time purchase through the Apple App Store or Google Play. Payment, billing, refunds and related data are handled by those stores under their own terms and privacy policies. The publisher has no access to your payment data.

6. When you write to us

If you contact support by e-mail, we use your e-mail address and the content of your message only to reply.

7. Children

The game collects no data whatever the player's age and contains no advertising or in-app purchase.

8. Your rights

Because the publisher processes no personal data through the game, there is nothing to access, correct or erase in that respect. For support messages (section 6) you may request access, correction or erasure by writing to romain.robert2@outlook.fr. You may also lodge a complaint with your national supervisory authority (in France: the CNIL, cnil.fr).

9. Changes

If a future version of the game processed data (for example by adding a third-party component), this policy would be updated before that version is released.
